# Scene pictures

`scene-night.jpg`, `scene-dawn.jpg`, `scene-water.jpg`, `scene-fog.jpg` and `scene-golden.jpg`, and
the cut-outs `scene-<name>-fg.webp` (the scenery with a transparent sky, drawn over the clock), come
from couch-tv (https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv, `tv-home/web/backgrounds`, MIT licence). They
were made with an AI image model from text only, are not real places, and are free to use.

The clock font, Antonio (`assets/fonts/Antonio.ttf`), is by Vernon Adams and is licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/Antonio-OFL.txt`).

`scene-island-<time>.jpg` and `scene-island-<time>-fg.webp` (morning, afternoon, evening and night of a pixel
art island) were made for this launcher with Google Gemini from text prompts, then cropped to 16:9, resized to
1920x1080, and the island and sea cut out from the sky.

`scene-adventure-<world>.jpg` and `scene-adventure-<world>-fg.webp` (a cartoon backyard in the morning, a toy
platform world in the afternoon and a block world at night) were made the same way, with Google Gemini from text
prompts, cropped, resized and cut out.
